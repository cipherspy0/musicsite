The Lost Christmas Eve
The Lost Christmas Eve is the fourth studio album by the Trans-Siberian Orchestra, released on October 12, 2004. It is the final installment in the group's holiday concept trilogy, preceded by Christmas Eve and Other Stories (1996) and The Christmas Attic (1998)

The Christmas Attic
The Christmas Attic is the third studio album by the Trans-Siberian Orchestra, released on October 13, 1998. It is the second installment in the group's holiday concept trilogy, preceded by Christmas Eve and Other Stories (1996) and followed by The Lost Christmas Eve (2004)

Dream Of Fireflies (On a Christmas Night)
Dreams of Fireflies (On a Christmas Night) is a cinematic, five-track holiday EP by the Trans-Siberian Orchestra that blends progressive rock, symphonic metal, and classical music. Released in October 2012 under the direction of late producer Paul O'Neill, the record serves as a short-form winter fantasy, featuring blistering guitar work, heavy synthesizers, and dramatic choral arrangements. The project is anchor-weighted by high-energy adaptations of classical themes—specifically drawing inspiration from Antonio Vivaldi's Winter and the works of Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart—before winding down into a gentle, lullaby-esque finale.

Christmas Eve and Other Stories
Christmas Eve and Other Stories is the second studio album by the Trans-Siberian Orchestra, released on October 15, 1996. It is the first installment in the group's holiday concept trilogy, followed by The Christmas Attic (1998) and The Lost Christmas Eve (2004)
