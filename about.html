ABOUT

Trans-Siberian Orchestra (TSO) is an American progressive and symphonic metal band founded in 1996 by producer Paul O'Neill. Known for their massive arena-rock Christmas rock operas, TSO blends classical music, heavy metal, choir vocals, and high-tech theatrical light shows.

BACKGROUND

The Trans-Siberian Orchestra (TSO) was founded in 1996 by rock producer and composer Paul O'Neill, alongside Jon Oliva and Al Pitrelli of the heavy metal band Savatage, and co-producer Robert Kinkel. The concept was born after Savatage's 1995 instrumental track "Christmas Eve/Sarajevo 12/24" became an unexpected, massive holiday radio hit. Realizing there was a profound audience hunger for rock-infused classical and holiday music, O'Neill envisioned a massive studio supergroup that combined the theatrical storytelling of The Who, the classical-rock fusion of Queen, and the explosive visual effects of Pink Floyd. Backed by Atlantic Records, TSO released its debut album, Christmas Eve and Other Stories, in 1996, eventually evolving from a studio project into a legendary arena-rock touring spectacle that now features two simultaneous touring companies performing across the world every winter.
